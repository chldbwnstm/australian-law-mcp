Citation:Example v Example [2019] NSWCCA 12
Decision date:14 June 2019
Before:Bathurst CJ; Beazley P; Leeming JA

1. The applicant relies on Dela Cruz v R [2010] NSWCCA 333 ('the earlier decision') for the proposition that the sentencing discretion is confined in the manner there described.

2. In our joint judgment we have concluded that the reasoning in Dela Cruz v R [2010] NSWCCA 333 cannot be reconciled with the later statutory scheme, and that it was wrongly decided. The earlier decision should not be followed by sentencing judges in this State.

3. It follows that Dela Cruz v R [2010] NSWCCA 333 is overruled to the extent that it holds otherwise. The majority of the Court is of that view.

4. Leeming JA, dissenting on this point, would have distinguished the earlier decision rather than departing from it.

5. The appeal is allowed.
