Offline fixtures for the statute tools (src/tools/*.test.ts).

Nothing in `npm test` touches the network. Every file below was recorded once
by hand with curl against the keyless public Federal Register endpoints
documented in docs/research/frl-api-reference.md, then trimmed.

All captures: 2026-09-04.
Host A: https://api.prod.legislation.gov.au/v1
Host B: https://www.legislation.gov.au


frl-search-cca.json
  GET A/Titles/Search(criteria='text("competition%20and%20consumer%20act",name,contains)')
      ?$top=5&$select=id,name,collection,subCollection,status,isPrincipal,isInForce,
       year,number,seriesType,nameHistory,statusHistory,hasCommencedUnincorporatedAmendments
      &$count=true
  Verbatim, untrimmed. Recorded because it IS the ranking problem: FRL relevance
  order puts a 2020 amending Act and two repealed price-notification instruments
  ABOVE C2004A00109 (the CCA itself, 4th). rankTitles has to fix that.

frl-title-tpa-rename.json
  GET A/Titles?$filter=name eq 'Trade Practices Act 1974'&$select=<as above>&$count=true
  Verbatim. Two facts live here: a name filter matches HISTORICAL names (the
  query is the old name, the returned `name` is "Competition and Consumer Act
  2010"), and `nameHistory[].start` is when each name BEGAN — the row for
  "Trade Practices Act 1974" starts 1974-08-24. It also carries the duplicated
  nameHistory row upstream serves, and hasCommencedUnincorporatedAmendments=true.

frl-title-asic1989-repealed.json
  GET A/Titles?$filter=contains(name,'Australian Securities and Investments
      Commission Act 1989')&$select=<as above>&$count=true
  Verbatim. A Repealed title whose statusHistory names the repealing Act
  (Corporations (Repeals, Consequentials and Transitionals) Act 2001, sch 1
  item 1, C2004A00823) — the "what replaced it" link.

frl-authorises-cca.json
  GET A/Titles/Search(criteria='and(authorises("C2004A00109"),
      collection(LegislativeInstrument),status(InForce))')
      ?$top=10&$select=id,name,collection,subCollection,status,year,number
      &$expand=authorisedBy&$count=true&$orderby=name asc
  Verbatim. THE three-tier discovery: `authorises(...)` is an undocumented
  criteria function (not in the reference doc) that returns the instruments made
  under an Act, and $expand=authorisedBy attaches the enabling provision
  ("s 172", "s 134(1) of sch 2"). 146 in force at capture time.

frl-authorisedby-ccr.json
  GET A/Titles?$filter=id eq 'F1996B01420'&$select=id,name,collection,
      subCollection,status&$expand=authorisedBy
  Verbatim. The converse edge for one instrument: Competition and Consumer
  Regulations 2010 → C2004A00109 s 172.

frl-versions-cca.json
  GET A/Versions?$filter=titleId eq 'C2004A00109'&$orderby=start desc
      &$top=6&$count=true
  Verbatim (210 versions upstream; the 6 newest kept). Shows the two traps the
  tools label: the newest rows are FUTURE commencements with registerId:null,
  and reasons[].affectedByTitle.provisions describes the AMENDING Act's items
  ("sch 1 (item 66)"), never the amended sections — which is why
  get_provision_history reads the endnotes instead.

cca-schedules.ncx
  GET B/C2004A00109/latest/latest/text/latest/epub/OEBPS/document.ncx
  Full response: 2,603 navPoints, 830 KB. Pruned to 37 navPoints; labels,
  content src values and element shapes are byte-identical to the capture,
  playOrder/id were renumbered over the pruned tree. Kept: Volume 1 → Part II →
  ss 17A/18/19 (body) and Volume 4 → Schedule 1, Schedule 2 (chapters, Part 2-1,
  ACL ss 18/19) and Endnotes. That is the sch-2-s-18-vs-s-18 collision plus
  enough schedule structure for item counts.

cca-endnote-amendments.html
  GET B/C2004A00109/latest/latest/text/latest/epub/OEBPS/document_4/document_4.html
  Full response 4.3 MB. Kept: the `Endnote 4—Amendment history` anchor paragraph
  plus two windows of the table, verbatim <p> elements — the body rows around
